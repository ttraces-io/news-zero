# Privacy Model & Data Handling Policy

This platform is engineered to deliver cybersecurity intelligence with **zero user tracking** and complete client-side autonomy.

## Non-Negotiable Privacy Guarantees

1. **Zero Tracking Beacons**: No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no advertising trackers, no telemetry scripts.
2. **Zero User Accounts**: No passwordless email login, no user profiling, no device fingerprinting.
3. **Local-Only Preferences**: Selected topics, country filters, and liked items are stored exclusively in your browser's `localStorage` (`liked:<content-id>`).
4. **Static Sharing**: Share links are rendered deterministically (`/s/<short-id>/`). No external URL-shortener APIs or click-tracking redirectors are used.
5. **Public Metadata Only**: Scrapers ingest only public RSS metadata, canonical URLs, titles, public excerpts, and publication dates. Full copyrighted text is never stored or scraped.
