# Welcome to the news-zero Documentation Wiki

`news-zero` is a privacy-preserving, zero-tracking, deterministic cybersecurity news and open research paper aggregation platform built by **ttraces-io**.

## 📖 Wiki Topics

- [[Source-Methodology]]: Source vetting rules, RSS ingestion pipelines, and 200-task matrix scout architecture.
- [[Privacy-and-Security-Model]]: Non-negotiable zero-tracking, zero-credential scanning, and Playwright unauthenticated validation rules.
- [[Freshness-Policy]]: Strict 60-day publication date filter and deduplication algorithms.
